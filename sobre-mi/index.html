About me

I studied Audiovisual Communication at the Complutense University of Madrid. I’m interested in how a story changes when you tell it in a reel, an interview or a podcast.

When I edit, I pay particular attention to how one shot leads into the next, when the music comes in and what sound can add to what’s on screen.

I use generative AI in my audiovisual projects to develop ideas, explore visual styles and create visual assets.

Creative practice

I work across editing, photography, sound and visual identity. I start by asking what the viewer should feel, understand or remember.

Story first

I use editing, design and sound to support the message.

Format and audience

I adapt pacing and format to the platform and the people who will see the piece.

Visual consistency

I keep image, sound and graphic identity coherent across formats.

Curiosity

I enjoy learning new tools and experimenting with formats.

Editing and mixing

Dialogue editing, music selection and sound support for audiovisual pieces.

Audio storytelling

I structure voice, music and atmosphere so listeners can follow the story from start to finish.

How I build a visual identity

Concept

I start by defining the tone and personality of the project.

Shape and typography

I explore forms, lettering and composition that work at small sizes as well as on screen.

Visual system

I apply the identity across covers, thumbnails, social media and moving graphics.
